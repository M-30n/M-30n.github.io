Passing: when the receiver icons appear, press the button shown on the receiver you want (A, B, R, or L) to throw to him.

These come from the earlier GBA Madden manuals, which the 07 version closely follows, so a few actions may differ slightly. The in-game tutorial is the final word. You can remap keys from the settings icon in the player's toolbar, and a plugged-in controller should be detected automatically.
